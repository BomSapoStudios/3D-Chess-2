1. OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3D Chess 2 extends traditional chess into a fully three-dimensional cubic
playing field. Instead of an 8x8 flat board, pieces occupy a 3D grid where
movement occurs along X, Y, and Z axes simultaneously. The game supports
2 to 6 players, multiple board sizes, AI opponents, timed play, and an
optional Chaos Mode that introduces random board rotations mid-game.

Board Sizes:
  • 4x4x4  — 64 cells  — 2 players
  • 6x6x6  — 216 cells — 2–4 players
  • 8x8x8  — 512 cells — 2–6 players

Player Colors:
  White, Black, Green, Purple, Yellow, Orange


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. THE 3D BOARD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Each cell on the board is identified by a 3-coordinate position (X, Y, Z).

  • X-axis — runs left to right
  • Y-axis — runs bottom to top
  • Z-axis — runs front to back

Each player "owns" one face of the cube and advances their pawns inward
toward the opposite face. The axis of advance depends on the player's color:

  Color          Advance Axis   Starting Face
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────
  White          +X             X = 0
  Black          -X             X = max
  Green          +Z             Z = 0
  Purple         -Z             Z = max
  Yellow         +Y             Y = 0
  Orange         -Y             Y = max

The board can be freely rotated by the player in 3D space for better
viewing angles using the board rotation controls.

3. PIECE TYPES & MOVEMENT RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All pieces may not jump over other pieces unless noted (Knights are the
exception). Captures happen by landing on an occupied enemy cell.

────────────────────────────────────────────
KING
────────────────────────────────────────────
Moves exactly 1 cell in any direction, including diagonals.
In 3D this means up to 26 possible adjacent cells.
The King may never move into check.
Loss of the King = elimination from the game.

────────────────────────────────────────────
QUEEN
────────────────────────────────────────────
Moves any number of cells in a straight line in any direction:
  • Along any axis (X, Y, Z) — same as a Rook
  • Along any diagonal — same as a Bishop (including 3D space diagonals)
Most powerful piece on the board.

────────────────────────────────────────────
ROOK
────────────────────────────────────────────
Moves any number of cells along a single axis:
  • Straight left/right (X-axis)
  • Straight up/down (Y-axis)
  • Straight forward/backward (Z-axis)
Cannot move diagonally.

────────────────────────────────────────────
BISHOP
────────────────────────────────────────────
Moves any number of cells diagonally. In 3D space there are multiple
diagonal directions, including "true" 3D diagonals (moving along two
or three axes simultaneously at equal step sizes).

────────────────────────────────────────────
KNIGHT
────────────────────────────────────────────
Moves in an L-shape adapted for 3D space:
  2 cells along one axis + 1 cell along a perpendicular axis
The Knight is the only piece that can jump over other pieces.
Many more L-shape combinations exist in 3D than in 2D.

────────────────────────────────────────────
PAWN
────────────────────────────────────────────
Movement:
  • Advances 1 cell forward along the player's advance axis
  • Cannot retreat

Capture:
  • Captures diagonally — any of the 8 cells perpendicular to the
    advance axis that are 1 step diagonally forward
  • Cannot capture straight ahead

Promotion:
  • When a Pawn reaches the far face of the board (opposite starting
    face), it promotes to: Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight
  • AI promotion: selected automatically based on game state and difficulty

4. CHECK, CHECKMATE & DRAW CONDITIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CHECK
  A King is in check when it is threatened by at least one enemy piece.
  The player must resolve check on their next move (move the King, block
  the threat, or capture the threatening piece).
  In multi-player games, a player may be simultaneously threatened by
  multiple opponents.

CHECKMATE (Loss Condition)
  A player is checkmated when their King is in check AND there are no
  legal moves that resolve the check.
  In a 2-player game, checkmate immediately ends the game.
  In multi-player games (3+), the checkmated player is eliminated and
  the game continues.

STALEMATE (Draw Condition)
  A player is not in check but has no legal moves. The game is drawn.

DRAW CONDITIONS
  1. Stalemate — no legal moves, not in check
  2. Insufficient Material — neither side has enough pieces to checkmate
  3. Threefold Repetition — the exact same board position has occurred
     three times during the game
  4. Draw Offer — one player offers a draw and the opponent accepts
  5. Time Expiration — if timed play is enabled and a player's clock
     reaches zero without triggering checkmate, outcome is configurable

5. MULTI-PLAYER RULES (3–6 PLAYERS)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Player Count → Board Size:
  2 players → 4x4x4 or larger
  3–4 players → 6x6x6 or larger
  5–6 players → 8x8x8

Turn Order:
  Players take turns in a fixed rotation: White → Black → Green →
  Purple → Yellow → Orange. Eliminated players are skipped automatically.

Elimination:
  When a player is checkmated, they are eliminated from the game.
  Their remaining pieces are removed from the board.

CONQUEST SYSTEM (4+ Player Games)
  When Player A checkmates Player B, Player B's pieces are NOT simply
  removed. Instead, all of Player B's surviving pieces transfer to
  Player A's army. This dramatically shifts the balance of power and
  rewards aggressive play.

Victory:
  The last player with a King remaining wins the game.

6. PIECE PLACEMENT PHASE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before gameplay begins, each player places their pieces from a piece tray
onto their starting face of the board.

  • Each player has a tray of pieces to place: King, Queen, Rooks, Bishops,
    Knights, and Pawns
  • Placement is restricted to the player's starting face (a single-cell-
    deep slice of the board)
  • All players place simultaneously (not turn-based)
  • A "Ready" button must be pressed once a player has placed all pieces
  • AI players place automatically
  • The game proceeds to the Playing phase once ALL players are ready

Piece Inventory per Player (on 4x4x4 board):
  1 King, 1 Queen, 2 Rooks, 2 Bishops, 2 Knights, 4 Pawns

Piece counts scale with board size for larger boards.

7. OPTIONAL FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

────────────────────────────────────────────
TIMED PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Each player has a configurable countdown clock (3–30 minutes)
  • The timer counts down only during the player's turn
  • Timer pauses during animations, Chaos rotations, and pawn promotion
  • A warning is displayed when under 1 minute remains
  • If a player's timer reaches zero, they forfeit the game

────────────────────────────────────────────
CHAOS MODE
────────────────────────────────────────────
  Chaos Mode introduces random Rubik's cube-style rotations of the board
  at a configurable interval (every 3–25 turns).

  Rotation Types:
    Face Rotation    — Rotate one outer face of the cube
    Layer Rotation   — Rotate one internal slice of the cube
    Diagonal Rotation — Rotate around a diagonal axis

  Effects:
    • All pieces on the affected cells are physically repositioned
    • The board state updates accordingly — pieces move with the rotation
    • Timers pause during rotation animations
    • Strategic planning must account for upcoming board disruptions

  Configuration:
    • Enable/disable in game setup
    • Set rotation interval (every N turns)

8. AI OPPONENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Difficulty Levels:
  Easy   — Search depth 1 (evaluates 1 move ahead)
  Medium — Search depth 2
  Hard   — Search depth 3

Algorithm:
  Minimax with alpha-beta pruning

Position Evaluation Criteria:
  • Material score (piece values)
  • Center control (cells closer to center score higher)
  • Piece coordination
  • Layer control
  • Game phase detection (opening, midgame, endgame)
  • Territory evaluation

AI also plays the placement phase, placing pieces strategically based on
the selected difficulty level.

9. IN-GAME ACTIONS & UI
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

During the game, players have access to the following actions and displays:

  Offer Draw        — Propose a draw to all remaining opponents
  Resign / Forfeit  — Voluntarily concede the game
  Undo Move         — Take back the last move (if enabled in settings)
  Pause             — Pause the game and open the in-game menu

UI Elements:
  Check Indicators   — Visual and audio alerts when a King is in check
  Move History       — A scrollable log of all moves made this game
  Timer Display      — Countdown clocks for each player (when timed)
  Piece Trays        — Shows captured pieces and pieces remaining
  Coordinate Labels  — Reference labels on board axes
  Board Rotation     — Drag or use controls to orbit the 3D board
  Promotion Dialog   — Appears when a Pawn reaches the promotion rank

Visual Feedback:
  • Valid move destinations are highlighted when a piece is selected
  • Threatening pieces are highlighted when a King is in check
  • Smooth 3D animations play for all piece movements
  • Chaos rotation is animated with the board visually rotating

10. ONLINE MULTIPLAYER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  • Players connect to a shared game server via Socket.IO
  • One player creates a room and receives a Room Code
  • Other players join using that Room Code
  • All game state is synchronized in real-time across all clients
  • Supports 2–6 human players across any combination of platforms
    (PC, Mac, iOS, Android, WebGL/browser)
  • Connection drops are handled with reconnection attempts
